How a pitch works

  1. Open a room and share the link or code with anyone you want in the audience.
  2. Fill in your pitch card, then pitch out loud for a minute or two.
  3. Answer one question from each investor, three times round.
  4. Take an offer or walk away, then read your report and send it to whoever needs it.

Every room is separate: your pitch and your report belong only to you. Rooms close when everyone has left.
